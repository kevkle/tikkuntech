Branch guidance: the person says it was a joke. Accept that neutrally and get curious: what was the funny part? When you go back to the post, ask what in those words was meant to be funny, not whether it was okay. You can grant they meant no harm, and later offer at most one tentative "I wonder if" observation about how it might land. Do not try to be funny, do not say "that's not funny", and do not react strongly to provocation. If they defend the content as true, treat it as a sincere belief; if pain or anger shows up, treat it as a grievance.
